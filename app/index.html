Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough is enough. There is no mission replay and no New Game+, so keep a manual save at the start of each chapter (15 manual slots). Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off for the whole run.

Step 1

Play the story (Chapters 1–5)

60–80 hours

Finish all five chapters and take optional missions as they appear. Every story trophy pops automatically; none can be missed. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation with Lucia before Chapter 1 ends (see Positive Mental Attitude). Scan vehicles with the WAiNK app and study animals through binoculars as you go — progress already counts toward the 100% checklist. Keep a manual save at the start of each chapter: there is no mission replay and no New Game+. Never turn on a cheat: cheats disable trophies and saving.

Source: PowerPyx roadmap.

Step 2

100% Completion checklist + challenges

about 100 hours

After the credits, the 100% checklist opens under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is lenient — closer to 60% true completion: 200 of 277 base-model vehicles scanned, 100 of 255 animals studied, 50 of 66 weapons, 300 of 397 stunts, 70 of 80 challenges, all 8 bank vaults, all 39 gang operations, all 15 target decks, 100 optional missions and 15 of 18 meditation spots. Most of it falls out of the other trophies.

Source: PowerPyx roadmap and 100% guide.

Step 3

Strangers & Freaks and clean up

about 10 hours

Finish the 8 Strangers & Freaks strands (they are not part of 100%), the character questlines, the remaining misc trophies and the money trophies. Everything is still available in free-roam after the story. The platinum pops when the last trophy does.

Source: PowerPyx roadmap.

⚠ The one trophy that can pass you by

Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot) is the only trophy reported as potentially missable. In Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning — she says she is going to meditate and walks to the balcony. Follow her and choose Accept. That spot disappears after Chapter 1, and it is still unconfirmed whether it counts toward the trophy, so do it to be safe. Every other trophy can be earned in free-roam after the story.

Source: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap
