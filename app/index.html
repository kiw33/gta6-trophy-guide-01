Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough is enough. There is no mission replay and no New Game+, so keep a manual save at the start of each chapter (15 manual slots). Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off for the whole run.

    ⚠ The one trophy that can pass you by

    Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot) is the only trophy reported as potentially missable. In Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning — she says she is going to meditate and walks to the balcony. Follow her and choose Accept. That spot disappears after Chapter 1, and it is still unconfirmed whether it counts toward the trophy, so do it to be safe. Every other trophy can be earned in free-roam after the story.

    Source: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap
