Platinum roadmap

One playthrough is enough, and everything is offline. Work the three steps in order; the cleanup at the end is where most of the hours go.

  1. Step 1 — Play the story

    Finish the story at your leisure. Twelve trophies pop on their own: the five chapter trophies, the final-mission gold, and six named story missions. Enjoy it first; the checklist opens after the credits.

    12 trophies · 1 gold, 11 bronze

  2. Step 2 — Strangers, activities & crime

    Close the stranger strands — Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and more — try all nine activity types, win your races and drone races, and work the shooting range and the stunts. Do the meditation spots now, before the story moves past them.

    25 trophies · 4 silver, 21 bronze

  3. Step 3 — Cleanup & 100% completion

    After the credits, open the 100% checklist (Touch Pad > Progress > 100% Completion) and work it line by line: card sets, WAiNK scans, challenges, garages and money. The A Long Road gold and the platinum land here.

    18 trophies · 1 platinum, 1 gold, 2 silver, 14 bronze

⚠ Missable warning — read this first

Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot) is the only trophy reported as possibly missable. One meditation spot is at Jason’s house in Chapter 1 and may not be reachable later, so meditate there while you still can. Every other trophy can be earned after the story in free roam.
